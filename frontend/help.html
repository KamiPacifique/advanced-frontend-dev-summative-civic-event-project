Help Center

How do I register for an event?
Open Events, choose an event and press Register. It then appears under My Registrations.
Can I cancel?
Yes — from the event page or My Registrations. A cancelled registration can’t be re-activated from your account.
How do I leave feedback?
Registered attendees can rate an event (1–5 stars) and leave a comment once per event. You can edit your review later.
Still stuck?
Email support@civicconnect.rw or call +250 788 123 456.

Accessibility

  • All text colour pairs meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast.
  • Every control is keyboard reachable with a visible focus ring; dialogs trap focus and close with Esc.
  • Press / anywhere to jump to search.
  • Audio announcements have a keyboard-operable player (Space, arrow keys, M) and a download option.
  • Promo videos show English captions by default and include a readable transcript.
  • Loading, success and error messages are announced to screen readers.

Privacy Policy

We store your name, email, event registrations and feedback to provide the service. Your sign-in is kept in your browser (until you close the tab, or longer if you choose “Keep me signed in”) and expires after one hour. Signing out removes it.

Terms of Use

Use CivicConnect respectfully. Feedback must be honest and free of abuse. Administrators may disable accounts that misuse the platform.
